Let's Talk at the Canteen! 💬

Key expressions used when ordering food & drinks

"Good morning!" Greeting
"What would you like?" Asking
"I'd like a pastel, please." Ordering
"Anything else?" Seller Check
"How much is it?" Price Enquiry
"Here you are. / Thank you!" Politeness

Model Dialogue

Seller: Hello! What would you like?

Customer: I'd like a pastel, please.

Seller: Anything else?

Customer: No, thank you. How much is it?

Seller: It's four thousand rupiah, please.

Customer: Here you are.

Seller: Thank you!

✨ Polite word tip: Always remember to say "please" and "thank you"!
